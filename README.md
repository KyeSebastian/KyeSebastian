## About Me

I am a computer science graduate with my focus on networking: how a computer operates, communicates, and lives on a network. The only way to learn is by doing it. Some people build a whole data center in their living space and call it a home lab. I run everything I need off a single ThinkPad, all written up on [my blog](https://kye-mora.com/blog), a place to build, break, and learn from my own mistakes: firewall policy, network segmentation, a SIEM correlating real alerts, and infrastructure I automate myself.

My foundation is built on Java and Python. Solving problems logically and understanding what's happening under the hood is where I naturally gravitate. Computer science courses have humbled me, I wasn't always the best student in the room, but I knew one thing: I was going to get it done. That discipline is what lets me adapt to whatever's actually in front of me. I'm CompTIA Security+ certified, with CCNA in progress, backed by real IT fundamentals: troubleshooting, documentation, and systems support. I don't have a data center of my own (yet), but what I do bring is an unbiased mindset, a fresh perspective, and the resilience to work through a problem until it's actually solved. Feel free to reach out, I'd love to talk.

**Certifications:** CompTIA Security+ | CCNA (in progress)
